# General information

Data from the Wilkinson pharmaceutical dataset (Wilkinson) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from Wilinson et al., (2022). Permission for redistribution was provided by the lead author (john.wilkinson@york.ac.uk) on behalf of the copyright holder (the University of York).
