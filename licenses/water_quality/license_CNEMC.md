# General information

Data from the China National Environmental Monitoring Centre (CNEMC) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from Lin et al. (2024) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
