# General information

Data from the Water Quality Portal (WQP) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from the United States Geological Survey portal (https://www.waterqualitydata.us/) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
