# General information

Data from the Global River Water Quality Archive (GRQA) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from Virro et al. (2021) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.


## References
