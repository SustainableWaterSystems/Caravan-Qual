# General information

Data from the Iran Water Resources Management Company (IWRMC; https://stu.wrm.ir) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from Zarei et al. (2025) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
