# General information

Data from the the GLObal RIver Chemistry Database (GLORICH) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from Hartmann et al. (2014) and is published under the [CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) license. Please note that while the paper was released in 2014, the dataset itself refers to a 2019 update.
