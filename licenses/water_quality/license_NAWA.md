# General information

Data from the National Surface Water Quality Monitoring Programme of Switzerland (NAWA; https://www.bafu.admin.ch/en/water) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from do Nascimento et al. (2025) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
