# General information

Data from the Canadian Environmental Sustainability Indicators (CESI) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from The Environment and Climate Change Canada Data Catalogue and is published under the Data Server End-use License of Environment and Climate Change Canada.
