# General information

Data from the UK Environment Agency (UK-EA) (water quality observations, monitoring station latitude and longitude) included in Caravan-Qual was retrieved from: https://environment.data.gov.uk/water-quality-beta and is published under the Open Government License for public sector information (https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) license.
