# General information

This data was originally published as the LamaH-Ice dataset by Helgason et al. (2023). Compared to the original LamaH-Ice, the Caravan extension does not include basins with a strong anthropogenic or natural influence on streamflow (18 basins). The Creative Commons Attribution NonCommercial 4.0 International License [CC-BY-NC-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) applies to the streamflow measurements.
