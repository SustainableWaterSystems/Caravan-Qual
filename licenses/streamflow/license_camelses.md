# General information

CAMELS-ES data (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the CAMELS-ES dataset by Newman et al. (2015) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.

**Note: The discharge records are taken from the "Anuario de Aforos" (https://ceh.cedex.es/anuarioaforos/default.asp), a public repository of hydrological records in Spain curated by CEDEX (Centro de Estudios y Experimentación de Obras Públicas) and supported by the gauging networks of the several water agencies in the country.
