# General information

CAMELS-NZ data (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the CAMELS-NZ dataset by Bushra et al. (2025) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
