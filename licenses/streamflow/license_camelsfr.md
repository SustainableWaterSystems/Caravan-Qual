# General information

CAMELS-FR data (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the CAMELS-FR dataset by Delaigue et al. (2025) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
