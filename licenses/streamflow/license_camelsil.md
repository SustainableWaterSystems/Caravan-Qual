# General information

Data from the Caravan Extension for Israel (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the dataset by Efrat (2025) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.

**Note: source datasets (e.g. streamflow data and basin shape files) are from the Israel Hydrological Service, Israel Water Authority. Data and maps are available (and updated occasionally) on the following websites (in Hebrew): https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/surface-water/govil-landing-page and
https://www.gov.il/he/departments/topics/water_authority_maps/govil-landing-page
