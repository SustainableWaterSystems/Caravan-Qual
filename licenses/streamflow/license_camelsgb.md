# General information

CAMELS-GB data (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the CAMELS-Gb dataset by Coxon et al. (2020) and is published under the terms of the [Open Government Licence v3](http://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence). Caravan therefore contains data supplied by Natural Environment Research Council.

**Note**: in Caravan-Qual, the `gauge_lat` and `gauge_lon` values used are computed from `gauge_easting` and `gauge_northing` in CAMELS-GB.
