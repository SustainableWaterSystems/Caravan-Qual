# General information

CAMELS-CH data (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the CAMELS-CH dataset by Höge et al. (2023) and is published under the [CC-BY-4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.

Note, compared to the original CAMELS-CH, Caravan-Qual does not include lake basins (33 basins) and two basins that are only regulatory channels for reservoir management (ids 2446 and 2447) are also excluded. Data used from therefore contains only stream and river basins.
