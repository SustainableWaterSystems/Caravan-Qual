# General information

CAMELS-CZ data (streamflow time series, catchment boundaries, and gauge latitude, longitude and gauge names) included in Caravan-Qual was originally published as part of the CAMELS-CZ dataset by Krejči, J. & Nearing, G. (2025) and is published under the [CC-BY-NC-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) license.
